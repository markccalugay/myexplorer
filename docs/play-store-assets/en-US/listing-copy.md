## Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
MyExplorer helps you organize multi-stop trips in one clear itinerary so your plan is ready before you head out.

Build your trip, your way
• Create and name a trip, or start a fresh itinerary anytime
• Add stops with a place name, address, and coordinates
• Reorder stops up or down to set your Start, intermediate Stops, and Destination

Save and reopen on your device
• Keep your draft and saved trips stored locally on your device
• Get an unsaved-changes prompt before switching trips so edits are not lost
• Reopen saved itineraries whenever you are ready to review or update them

Route summary support
• View route status, total distance, estimated duration, and leg count for multi-stop itineraries when connected to a configured routing service

Designed for clarity
• Supports both light and dark system appearance
• Clean, focused Trip Planner layout built for quick pre-trip organization
```

- **Live turn-by-turn navigation / voice guidance** — Not implemented in `apps/mobile`.
